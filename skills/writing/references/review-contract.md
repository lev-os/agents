# Editorial review

Reject an unsupported claim before comparing style. A strong phrase does not
compensate for a factual defect, and accurate metadata does not prove a useful
article. Use quoted passages and source facts, not self-assigned numeric scores.

| Lane | Evidence needed for a pass |
|---|---|
| Fidelity | Every material claim resolves to evidence at its actual strength; dates, quantities, quotations, conditions and limitations survive the edit. |
| Contribution | The declared genre delivers a useful explanation, judgment, calculation, comparison, synthesis or verified experience; the model invents no institutional position. |
| Usefulness | The draft itself supplies one reader, situation, changed decision or understanding, evidence chain and earned conclusion. |
| Title promise | Subject and payoff make sense without the source; the first two paragraphs and conclusion deliver them; adjacent titles do not repeat one mechanic by reflex. |
| Voice and compression | The approved voice survives, paragraphs each do useful work, necessary caveats remain, and the form fits the argument. |

For variants, withhold generator identities, self-scores and prior approval.
Screen the complete headline/deck/opening unit against the source; then compare
readability, relevance, specificity and interest. Return winner and runner-up
with reasons, or reject-all. Selection is provisional until the complete body
pays the promise. A fresh final editor should not inherit the shortlist's scores
or selection rationale. Preserve owner rejection even when models prefer a draft.

Verdicts: `pass` when every applicable lane passes; `repair` when the premise
survives and a failed lane has a concrete return step; `no-publish` when no useful
supported premise survives; `blocked` when required evidence or authority is
missing. None of these changes publication status or authorizes external action.
