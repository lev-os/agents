# Pilot training

For aviation learning-resource discovery, inspect `lev research strategies --json`
for a live `pilot-training` strategy before using it. If absent, use the ordinary
deep routine with explicit training subquestions and report that mapping.
Preserve learner testimony, learning styles, free videos/materials, ATC phraseology,
source dates and licenses where corpus reuse is contemplated. Discovery does not
qualify a flight runtime, canonical corpus, sealed acceptance set or training claim.
