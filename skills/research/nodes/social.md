# Social

1. Run `lev research <query> -s social --no-wikify` across its fixed social adapters.
2. Normalize posts by platform, author, timestamp, URL, and engagement.
3. Deduplicate reposts and separate observed sentiment from factual claims.
4. Corroborate material factual claims through the quick web routine.
5. Return platform coverage, sentiment, corroborated facts, source URLs, and gaps. Stop.
