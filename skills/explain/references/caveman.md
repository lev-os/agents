# Explain: ELI5 and Caveman modes

This adaptation is explanation-scoped; it installs no plugin, proxy, hook or persistent mode.

## ELI5
Explain the purpose using familiar words. Introduce one concept at a time, with a concrete everyday example where it helps. Connect the example to the real mechanism and state its limits. Explain unfamiliar terms on first use. Keep full sentences and causal links when they improve understanding. Do not assume the reader knows DB/API acronyms. Cut filler after the explanation works; fragments or dropped articles are never a success criterion.

## Concise Caveman
Answer directly, remove filler and repeated facts, use active voice and one idea per sentence. Familiar technical terms can remain. Avoid invented abbreviations; clarity wins over compression. Keep uncertainty that affects the conclusion. Do not mangle grammar to imitate a persona.

## Shared preservation/check
Keep code, commands, paths, API names, exact errors, numbers and units verbatim. Keep negation, exclusivity, exceptions, ordering, decision owners and required details. Use clear complete sentences for warnings, consequential decisions, multi-step procedures or any ambiguity. Preserve the user's language. Persisted documents and third-party text use normal prose unless expressly requested otherwise.

Before finishing, ask: can the reader explain the mechanism and act safely; are all exact strings unchanged; did brevity hide a cause, caveat or necessary step? If the user asks again, expand the explanation. Normal Explain retains its source/decision discipline and no mode persists automatically.

## Adaptation dispositions
Represented: filler removal, plain consistent vocabulary, active voice, clarity override, technical-string preservation and normal persisted prose. Superseded: always-on persistence and no-progress/tool narration (Explain scope and host rules govern). Qualified: article deletion/fragments and token-saving claims. Excluded: wenyan variants, plugin/proxy/runtime integration and automatic hooks; outside the requested English explanation modes.
