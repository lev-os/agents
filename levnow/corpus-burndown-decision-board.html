DECISION 1 — THE BIG ONE

The advisory_ack convention

What does the FMM receive when ATC says something that isn't an instruction?

Model the reality: ATC says "N17XX, radar contact." In the air there is nothing to comply with — the pilot acknowledges at most ("roger") and reads nothing back. But the flight manager plausibly does need to know radar services are established; it's situational state, not a command.

The newer 122 rows encode exactly that: the parser emits the intent, the readback is roger/none, and the FMM receives a non-executable metadata command tagged advisory_ack, which the fake FMM accepts via the parameterless-intent policy landed at tick 40. The older 20 rows predate this convention: same utterances, but they expect the FMM to receive nothing at all.

No runtime-legal signal distinguishes the two populations. The split correlates with scenario authoring vintage — the 20 exclusion rows look like stale gold, not a deliberate rule.

✅ Option A — Adopt advisory_ack everywhere  (recommended)

Ruling: advisories and acknowledgments DO reach the FMM, as non-executable advisory_ack metadata.

  • Matches the reality model: the FMM should know radar services are active.
  • Zero runtime change for this population — the pipeline already emits these commands and the typed policy already accepts them.
  • Work: re-ground the 20 stale rows (gold edits with FAA receipts, human-approved) — mostly holding_pattern_clearance and request_direct_routing.
  • Keeps 122 currently-green rows green.

Option B — Exclusion principle

Ruling: ack/advisory-class utterances never become FMM proposals.

  • Requires a runtime assembly change to drop these commands.
  • Requires re-grounding 122 rows the other way — 6× the gold churn.
  • Regresses rows that are green today (verified against the tick-50 receipt).
  • Weaker reality model: the flight manager stays blind to radar/advisory state.

Recommendation: Option A

The 122-row convention is the one the reality model, the landed typed policy, and the anti-cheat advisory route all already agree with. Your ruling here unblocks the compound FMM lane: the failing hold rows, the radar-contact compounds, and the 360_for_spacing informational cases all sit behind it.
