PROGRAMS

The key salt was random and never saved, so a second process could not decrypt what the first stored. Two held patches: save the salt in a header, then read CLI arguments with the secret on stdin only. The real vault was never touched.

The registry check has a list of known broken commands that may only shrink; fixes for discourse-graph (6) and index refresh have landed as held patches. Decision 9.
