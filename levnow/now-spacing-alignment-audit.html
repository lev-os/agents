Coverage failure

The suite is broad by page count, shallow by component state

What the 40 pages do cover

21 of 22 element types appear somewhere. Both hero, feedback, and navigation variants are present. All eight page families render without horizontal overflow or console errors.

What they do not cover

11 of 13 section variants; 10 of 11 card variants; 5 of 6 text variants; 2 of 3 action variants; status-badge; custom-html; and 3 of 4 chart types. Card and text are never exercised as direct root siblings.

Why QA missed it

The checks proved function, not composition quality

The green checks

Schema admission, rendering, responsive overflow, console safety, interaction behavior, and one screenshot per page/width all passed. Those are still valid functional results.

The missing checks

The saved screenshot was only the first viewport. No assertion measured sibling gap, overlap, alignment rail changes, common layout classes, or full-page chunk coverage. MIX-02's lower seams were never judged.

Bounded conclusion

This is not primarily an agent choosing left alignment.

Evidence: Every rendered page uses a block container with row-gap normal; root HTML fragments are concatenated; all root elements share only the animation class; section children alone receive parent-owned gap rules; and the acceptance corpus records failures in every family that freely mixes root peers.
